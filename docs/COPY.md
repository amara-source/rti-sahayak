## About

Eyebrow: About RTI Sahayak

Heading: The legal right, with a guide beside you

Introduction: RTI Sahayak is a concept prototype that helps a citizen file a
Right to Information request correctly and track the legal clock afterwards.

Section: Why it exists

- A simple right became a paid service. The RTI Act gives every citizen a ten
  rupee right to ask for public records. A paid industry has grown up charging
  hundreds to use it because the government's own portal takes a form and a
  payment, but does not guide the citizen through the legal process.
- The clock should not be hidden. Filing is only the beginning. RTI Sahayak
  keeps the reply period, deemed refusal, First Appeal and Second Appeal
  visible as one continuous case.
- A prototype, not an authority. This is not an official product, is not
  affiliated with the Government of India and is not connected to any live
  government system.

Section: The two websites citizens need

- rti.gov.in. The RTI Portal Gateway explains the Act and holds the directory
  of Public Information Officers and Appellate Authorities.
- rtionline.gov.in. RTI Online is where central government applications are
  filed and paid for, and where their status and legal clock are tracked.

## Privacy

Heading: Stored in your browser, not in an account

The prototype stores one case in a browser cookie for seven days. It creates
no account and no server-side personal-data record. When writing assistance is
available, request text is processed transiently and is not stored by RTI
Sahayak. Nothing is submitted to a government system.

## Terms

Heading: A prototype, not legal advice

The explanations are educational, there is no guarantee of an outcome, and
all OTP, payment, registration, department and appeal events are simulated.

Checks: Wrong government, too much text, unsupported characters, several
